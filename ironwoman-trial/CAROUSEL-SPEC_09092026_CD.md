# drCWDugan carousel specification

Paste this whole file into Claude Design, together with the three brand files named at the bottom. Written 2026-09-09. Every number in it was measured out of the live IRONWOMAN deck, `IRONWOMAN Carousel Rebuild SOURCE_09082026_CD.dc.html`, not remembered.

## Canvas

```
artboard        2160 x 2700 px        4:5, the Instagram portrait ratio
padding         176px on all four sides, every artboard, no exceptions
surface         ink. Field is #0B0B0C
slides          8, and the counter reads N / 8
```

A 2160px canvas renders near 400 CSS px in a phone feed, so treat every size below as roughly a fifth of what it looks like on your screen.

## The six-part anatomy, and it is the same on every slide

Every artboard has EXACTLY these six top-level children, stacked, in this order. This is the structure that makes eight slides read as one deck.

```
1  HEADER ROW      the lockup on the left, the N / 8 counter on the right
                   one row, align-items:flex-start, so both sit on a shared
                   top edge. Do NOT use align-items:center, which centres each
                   column on its own height and puts them 10px out.
2  HAIRLINE RULE   1px, #2A2A2D, full content width
3  TITLE BLOCK     padding-top:64px
4  CONTENT BLOCK   flex:1 and justify-content:center. This is what makes the
                   picture sit optically centred whatever its height, and it
                   is the single most-broken part of the layout
5  KICKER          the one-line payoff, serif italic
6  FOOTER          citation on the left, handle on the right
```

If a slide does not have six top-level children, it is broken, whatever it looks like. That test is what has caught every layout bug in this deck.

## Type

```
display          DM Serif Display
body and labels  DM Sans
figures, units   IBM Plex Sans
```

Measured sizes actually in use on the 2160px canvas:

```
eyebrow, small caps      34px, letter-spacing 0.18em, line-height 1.3
label, meta              30px
kicker, body             50px
headline, secondary      56px
headline, primary        64px
headline letter-spacing  -0.02em
headline line-height     1.1 to 1.15
gaps between items       22px or 36px
```

MINIMUM READABLE SIZE IS 32px on a 1080px canvas, which is 64px here. Anything smaller is decoration and gets cut rather than shrunk. That includes citations and confidence intervals.

## Colour

```
field             #0B0B0C
primary text      #FFFFFF
secondary text    #E4E5E7
muted, labels     #C8CACE
hairlines, rules  #2A2A2D
accent            #d8cfe7      the ONE thing that matters per slide
eyebrow, caps     #8a6fb8
```

Grape is an accent and an ink, never a background and never a large area of colour. Neutrals carry more than ninety percent of every slide. Keep 35 to 40 percent of the artboard as clear field, measured as layout negative space and not as a count of background pixels.

## Hard rules that have each cost a rebuild

**One idea per slide.** Each card is one number and one shape. If a card needs two sentences to explain itself it is two cards.

**Line art only.** Strokes 4 to 6px on a 1080 canvas. No gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D, no glow, no bevels. Exactly one element per slide may be filled solid, and that element is the point of the slide.

**A component never hard-codes a colour.** It reads a surface token. Reading a raw palette value directly is what makes an asset unportable between the paper and ink surfaces.

**Never size a text box by multiplying character count by a per-character constant.** That under-measures real glyph advance and the longest strings overflow. Let the text lay out its own box so overflow is structurally impossible, and verify every box at several widths.

**Australian spelling.** Haemoglobin, not hemoglobin.

**No em dashes, no en dashes.** Use a comma, parentheses, or a full stop.

**Banned words in any on-screen text:** prove, proven, fact, absolute. No exclamation marks. No emoji.

**Micrograms per litre is written ug/L**, never the micro sign, because an uppercase transform maps it to a capital Greek mu and it ships as a thousandfold unit error.

## Slide roles across an eight-slide deck

Taken from the IRONWOMAN deck as built:

```
1  title, and the study design as a diagram
2  the two-column comparison, what changed and what did not
3  the primary chart
4  the individual-level picture, where the variance lives
5  three cards, the practical reading
6  what to expect
7  the single instruction
8  the source, and the claim that every number is in the paper
```

## What is not covered here

This spec describes the INK surface, which is what the IRONWOMAN deck uses. The paper surface is a full peer and has its own values in the brand card. Set the surface on the card root, never per component.
