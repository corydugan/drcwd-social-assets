REBUILD FOUR SLIDES OF AN EXISTING NINE SLIDE CAROUSEL

Built 2026-09-08. This replaces slides 1, 2, 6 and 7 of the IRONWOMAN carousel. Slides 3, 4, 5, 8 and 9 are FINISHED and are not being rebuilt. The four new slides have to sit beside them without anyone being able to tell which five are older.

UPLOAD THESE FIVE AS REFERENCE BEFORE YOU START: slide3.png, slide4.png, slide5.png, slide8.png, slide9.png. Match their layout, spacing, type and colour exactly. Where this brief and the reference slides disagree, the reference slides win.

TWO THINGS THAT MUST NOT APPEAR, AND THIS IS THE WHOLE REASON FOR THE REBUILD

1. Nothing that tells a reader not to use an exercise or fitness test. No "do not use", no veto of any measurement.
2. Nothing negative about exercise performance. No "null result", no "did not change", no "did not move", no "not a performance drug", no "the parts that did not work". The trial's published conclusion is positive about exercise performance and the slides must read that way: "intravenous iron therapy corrects iron status and improves both exercise economy and fatigue scores".

CANVAS AND SURFACE

Canvas 2160 x 2700, which is 4:5 portrait. The surface is the drCWDugan ink surface, not a dark mode variant of the paper one. Field #0B0B0C. Foreground #FFFFFF. Muted foreground #E4E5E7. Subtle foreground #C8CACE. Hairline rules #2A2A2D. Eyebrow small caps #8a6fb8. Accent, used for the italic words in a headline and for any filled element, #d8cfe7. There is no gradient anywhere and grape is never the field.

TYPE

Display headlines and the bottom kicker are DM Serif Display. Body text, eyebrows and labels are DM Sans. Any figure, statistic or n value is IBM Plex Sans. Display tracking is -0.02em. Body line height 1.6. Nothing that is meant to be read sits below 32px on a 1080px canvas, which is 64px on this 2160px one.

THE ANATOMY EVERY SLIDE SHARES, TAKEN FROM THE REFERENCE FIVE

Top left carries a small solid triangle followed by the wordmark drCWDugan in serif. Top right carries the slide number as "N / 9" in small muted sans. A hairline rule runs under that header band. The lower third stays largely empty, because the layout is field dominant and needs 35 to 40 per cent clear field. A kicker sits near the bottom, centred, serif italic. The footer runs "Dugan et al., Br J Sports Med 2025." on the left in small muted sans and "@dr.cwdugan" on the right in small bold sans.

VOICE AND MECHANICS

Australian English. First person singular where a voice appears at all. No em dashes and no en dashes as prose punctuation, use a comma or a full stop. The words prove, proven, fact and absolute are banned in rendered slide text. No exclamation marks. No emoji. Write micrograms per litre as ug/L and never as the micro sign, because uppercasing turns the micro sign into a capital Greek mu and that is a thousandfold unit error nobody sees in the source.

WHAT NOT TO DO

Do not renumber the slides. They are 1, 2, 6 and 7 of 9 and the counter in the top right has to say so. Do not add a slide. Do not restyle the footer or the header. Do not introduce a colour that is not in the list above. Do not fill the lower third; the empty space is the design. Do not put a number on a slide that is not in the paper.
