## The thing to get right

Every beat contains motion for its whole length. Never reach the end of a beat's movement and then hold a finished frame while the timeline catches up. There must never be a stretch where the words have stopped and the picture is still going, and never a stretch where the picture is idle while the words run.

## Unchanged constraints, restated so nothing drifts

Field #0B0B0C. Text #FFFFFF, secondary #E4E5E7, muted #C8CACE, hairlines #2A2A2D, accent #D8CFE7, eyebrow #8A6FB8. Purple is ink and accent only, never a background fill.

DM Serif Display for display, DM Sans for body and labels, IBM Plex Sans for any figure or unit. Line art, strokes 4 to 6 pixels, no gradients, no shadows, no glow. One filled element per beat at most.

Minimum readable size 32 pixels on the 1080 wide canvas. Let text lay out its own box rather than estimating width from character count.

Australian spelling. No em dashes or en dashes. No exclamation marks. The words prove, proven, fact and absolute are banned from on-screen text. No numbers, prevalences or thresholds on screen, except the panel row numbers 01 to 11 in the opening beat.

This episode stands completely alone. Nothing may assume another episode was watched.
