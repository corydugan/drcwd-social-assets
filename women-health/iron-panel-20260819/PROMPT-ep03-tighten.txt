## The thing to get right

Every beat must contain motion for its whole length. Do not reach the end of a beat's movement and then hold a finished frame while the timeline catches up. A shorter script means each beat has less time, so the movement inside it should be correspondingly quicker and tighter, not the same movement followed by a pause. If a beat is 3.8 seconds, something should be moving for all 3.8 of them.

There must never be a stretch where the words have stopped and the picture is still going, and never a stretch where the picture is idle while the words run.
